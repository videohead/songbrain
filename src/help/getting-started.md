# Welcome to Song Brain

Song Brain is built around one idea: **the chord chart is the vibe.** Playback, MIDI, MusicXML and the lead sheet are all derived from the chords you enter, and the AI agent edits the same chart you do.

## The screen

- **Top bar:** undo and redo, **Train style**, this Help (?) and the **agent** button (the three-dot network icon) that shows or hides the agent panel.
- **Transport bar:** open or save a song, play controls, the song's title, key, tempo, meter and default style, plus the **Mixer** for instruments and MIDI output.
- **Chord grid:** your song as sections of bars. Switch to **Lead sheet** for a notation view of the same chart.

## Quick start

1. Open **Quick-start charts** in this Help and press **Load** on any chart.
2. Press **▶** (or Space) to hear it.
3. Change a chord: double-click a bar, type a new chord, press Enter. The change plays straight away.
4. Pick a different **Style** in the transport bar to change the feel without touching the chords.

Everything is undoable with Ctrl+Z (Ctrl+Shift+Z to redo), so it is safe to experiment.

## Keyboard shortcuts

- **Space:** play or pause. **Esc:** stop. **Home** and **End:** jump to the start or end. **,** and **.:** previous or next bar.
- **Arrow keys:** move the selected bar. Add **Shift** to select a range.
- **Enter:** edit the selected bar. **Delete:** remove the selected bars.
- **Ctrl+C** and **Ctrl+V:** copy and paste bars.

## Saving and sharing

Use the save buttons next to **Open…** to export **JSON** (the full Song Brain file), **MIDI** or **MusicXML**. **Open…** reads Song Brain JSON and MusicXML lead sheets back in.
